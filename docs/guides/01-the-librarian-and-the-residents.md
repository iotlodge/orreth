# Guide 01 — The Librarian & the Residents, by example

*First edition 2026-07-11 (Fable 5). Everything below is live on the dev rig —
open the Console (`http://localhost:4500/window`, or any floor's port), click a
resident in the left rail, and the parlor opens. These are the flows, spoken the
way you actually speak them.*

---

## The one law to hold onto

**You never read the universe; you ask it.** A resident receives your ask, fetches
with its OWN authority, and answers on the record — the exchange lands as a signed
memory. That's why every example here is a sentence, not a query language.

---

## 1. The Librarian — knowledge, the portrait, and moments

The librarian is one mind with a seat on every floor. Click her, and her calling
card offers chips; or type any of these:

### Gather knowledge
> **you:** `gather sourced knowledge on cold-weather build strategies`
> **her:** *"gathering on 'cold-weather build strategies' — sourced findings land in
> the Window, quarantined until corroborated."*

What happens: she finds a **serving** search source on the Farm (never an
unattested one), meters the call, and admits each finding as a signed memory under
the source's DID — at confidence **0.0000**, untrusted. The world speaks at zero;
promotion is earned by corroboration. If a discredited source is serving, she
refuses at the door — the universe remembers the infection (0026 §5).

### Ask the whole universe
> **you:** `ask the universe about payment compliance`
> **her:** *"asking my seats about 'payment compliance' — every floor's librarian
> answers from what its own seat may read…"*

One mind, many seats: a leg stages on every other floor's queue, each seat answers
from its own floor with its own key, and the composed answer names each seat — and
is honest about the dark ones. Recalled lineages stay dead; sealed refs stay dark.

### Your living portrait (0025)
> **you:** `my profile: I toast with cocoa at midnight` → enters **trusted** (you
> are sovereign over you)
> **you:** `what do you know about me` → *"you told me: … · I observed (untrusted
> until corroborated): …"* — every stroke wears its receipt
> **you:** `forget about me: cocoa` → the claims go silent NOW (lineage-death) and
> their bytes leave the store through the tombstone door (0026); the signed stubs
> and the withdrawal stay — THAT you forgot is on the record, WHAT you forgot no
> longer exists.

### Mark a moment (0024)
> **you:** `remember this: the demo went perfectly as major`
> **her:** *"remembered as a major moment…"* — a signed life-event marker, derived
> from this very audience. Major and substantial moments become her (untrusted)
> observations on your portrait.

### The workspace (0028)
Hit **⛶** in the parlor header: her room opens — the shelf's counts, your portrait,
and the moments & markers panel where anything graded medium+ wears **amber**
(pending co-review is visible at a glance).

## 2. becky — the door, and growing the universe

- `who holds a lease on this floor?` — the roster of granted leases, root-chained.
- `how does an agent join?` — the door's law: DID presented, nonce challenge
  (key control proven), the ask sits human-visible in the queue, and becky alone
  mints the lease — after YOU approve. Consequence waits.
- `create ecosystem retail with fields web, pos` — the Shipyard drafts a launch
  plan and stages it for you; on approval, real containers rise on the rig, pull
  their parent's floors at boot, and beat into the orrery by themselves.

## 3. charlotte — the farm keeper

- `what is serving right now?` — the toolshed roster; every serving service wears
  a pinned manifest.
- `is anything in quarantine?` — a service that came back **changed** walks the
  rug-pull door: nothing serves until a human re-opens the gate.
- Discrediting a source at the gate hands the librarian a **recall walk**: every
  entry that source fed — and everything derived from those, however deep — is
  re-versioned to `recalled`. Annotated, never rewritten; the poison visibly dead.

## 4. ada — the wrangler

- `which minds serve this floor?` · `what does thinking cost here?` · `what
  expires soon?` — the stable, the meter (per agent, residents included, honest
  zeros shown), and the pasture calendar. Price drift under a pinned deal comes
  back to you as a decision, never an outage.

## 5. The unembodied organs

vigil (the Warden), steward (memory), governance (floors) receive you politely and
say honestly that they have no voice yet — their seats are reserved. They still
work: vigil watches shape (never content), the steward distills what rises,
governance now grades improvement proposals (0028).

## 6. The standing residents you don't talk to (yet)

- **The portfolio monitor** (0027) beats on the universe floor — an immortal job
  writing aggregate observations. No completion condition exists.
- **The improver** (0028) reads the receipts — rollups, markers, parked intents —
  and proposes sibling versions of behavioral assets. Governance grades the change
  **by diff**: a parameter nudge adopts loudly on the medium lane; a rewrite waits
  in the queue for YOU.

## 7. An objective, end to end (0027)

Post an objective (today via the queue; the Console UX rides a later pass):

```
POST /requests  {"kind": "objective", "text": "build me a product that does FOO"}
```

The orchestration seat plans HIGH and dispatches **slivers** down the request
queue — each floor's fingertip receives one intent, a budget slice, and refs,
never the whole. Mid-flow, the IaC branch asks YOU a question (`kind:"question"`
in the queue — resolve it with your answer; silence is denial). Every return is
graded with a critic marker; the assembly resolves YOUR request — the human's
request is the top node, and nothing ever confirms its own completion.

## 8. The purge — when a memory must end (0026)

Three endings, all on the record: **recalled** (visible and dead), **withdrawn**
(silent by your consent, bytes shredded on the consent path), **purged**
(operational: `kind:"purge"` stages, the seal darkens the refs at machine speed,
and destruction HOLDS — *"quorum 1 of 2 — containment active; destruction waits
for humans, plural"*). A solo universe can contain forever; it destroys only when
quorum exists.
