## 1. The seed (JB, 2026-08-03, found by his own testing)

"List all skills on the shelves" earned canned status cards from both the
librarian and grace. The parlor ROUTES keywords; it does not LISTEN.
Humans must speak normal language to residents and get real answers,
grounded within — and where legitimate, across — their scopes. Quick-launch
chips stay. Persona/profiles deliberately wait for the Profile
capabilities. This dive is the fluid feel of UI interoperability itself.

## 2. The efficient shape — one path, pieces we already own

The insight that keeps this dive LITTLE: nothing new is invented.

1. **The voiced lane becomes the default.** 0020's machinery already runs
   a metered thought under each resident's own DID, speaking the
   `resident-voice` CANON firmware (readable and releasable in the Craft
   Room since 0045). Today it fires only behind keyword routing; now every
   non-protocol utterance takes it. Protocol words (dial-set and kin) stay
   FIRST as verbatim regex — the 0021 law: voiced replies never rewrite
   flow-control words. Templates remain as the honest no-mind fallback.
2. **Grounding kits, per resident — from the CACHED composers.** One
   registry: `GROUNDING = {name: facts_fn}`. Each returns ≤ ~1,500 chars
   of live truth composed mostly from payloads the worker already caches:
   `/governance` (the registry — THE CROSS-SCOPE LIBRARY CARD: skills and
   craft across floors, rule-7-clean) · `/observatory` (vera's numbers,
   the dial, the bell's state) · the farm roster · the stable ledger · the
   consent heads. Grounding a chat costs approximately nothing beyond the
   thought itself.
3. **Scope-honesty as firmware, shipped through the ceremony.** The
   `resident-voice` prompt gains one released sentence: *when the question
   falls outside your charter, say so in one sentence and name the
   resident who keeps it.* That release is a real 0045 sp3 ceremony — the
   human's click renames the machine — and referral becomes law, never
   improvisation.
4. **Persona later, for free.** When Profiles land, a persona is
   per-resident Chronicle craft worn via `craft()` — editable in
   Governance under the one law. No new mechanism will be needed.
5. **The cost is governed and visible**: one medium thought per utterance
   (~240 tokens ≈ $0.002), metered under the speaking resident's DID —
   the rail and Pulse already show it.
