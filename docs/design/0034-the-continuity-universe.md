runs a SOX-governed enterprise runs a bedside companion — different retention
regimes, different distortion contracts (0033 §5), same physics, same covenant.

## 3. Truth-telling to a vulnerable human — the label canon

The system's most important sentence shape: honest confidence, in human words.
The fidelity ladder already exists on every record; this dive locks the **spoken
presentation** of it:

```text
verified / human-confirmed   →  "On May 12th you..."           (say it plainly)
corroborated (sources named) →  "The photos and your calendar
                                 show..."                       (show the receipts)
untrusted / inferred         →  "This MAY have happened — I
                                 have two hints, not proof."    (hedge, honestly)
investigating (0031 §5)      →  "I'm re-checking that one."     (doubt out loud)
recalled                     →  never spoken as memory; only as
                                 "something I was told and later
                                 learned was wrong," on request
```

A resident may say *"I found a photo and two calendar entries suggesting this may
have happened in May 2024."* It must never say *"You definitely did this"* above
its evidence. **The prohibition is structural, not behavioral**: the reply
composer reads the state off the record — a mind cannot upgrade confidence the
substrate doesn't hold (0016/covenant 5: composition happens where the records
are).

Authority types ride as tags on existing claims — `human-stated ·
human-confirmed · caregiver-supplied · document-verified · clinician-verified ·
inferred` — no new schema; the GIN indexes already serve them (0022).

## 4. Consent, delegation, and the universe that outlives its human

- **Consent as dynamic state:** becky's capability grants gain purpose + modality
  + validity-window + revocation on the personal template. Degradation is a
  posture: consent withdrawn ⇒ the affected organs fall to a *safer mode*
  (recording stops; recall of already-consented history follows its own grant).
  0026's split model (consent path vs operational path) already carries the
  erasure half.
- **Delegated authority:** roles (partner · caregiver · clinician · guardian ·
  emergency responder · technician) are entitlement bundles over memory domains
  — a caregiver sees routines and medication support, never journals or sealed
  classes. 0013's governed-principal machinery serves this today; the vocabulary
  and default bundles are the new artifact.
- **Survivorship (OPEN, and handled with gravity):** what happens when the root
  authority dies? Options staged for JB, no default assumed: the universe seals
  (read-only legacy, executor-gated) · passes by pre-signed succession grant ·
  crypto-shreds on a dead-man protocol · or a governed mix per memory domain.
  This is 0012's quorum thinking applied to mortality, and it deserves its own
  lock — possibly its own dive.

## 5. The Brain Glass — JB's visualization, made a projection

The concept (JB's images, `docs/vision/brain-glass-concept-{1,2,3}.png`): **the
universe rendered as a brain** — and crucially, *laid out like one*:

- **Ecosystems as mapped regions** — a functional placement table (proposed,
  JB tunes): memory ecosystem → temporal/hippocampal territory · governance/
  consent → prefrontal · environmental & sensory → occipital-parietal ·
  procedural/routine → cerebellar · identity/relationships → the limbic core.
  Fields cluster inside their region like gyri.
- **Activity is firing.** Every IAC/agentic act already beats up the heartbeat
  chain — the Brain Glass renders those beats as neurons firing along the
  region's paths: an intention fanning = a signal crossing regions; a gather =
  sensory cortex lighting; an adoption at grace's gate = prefrontal flare;
  memory formation = a glow that *persists* where the record landed.
- **JB's pattern hypothesis, honestly framed:** if placement is functional, then
  *where light accumulates is what the universe is doing with its life* — usage
  patterns become spatially legible and comparable across universes (an
  enterprise brain lights differently than a continuity brain — that contrast IS
  the demo). The claim is **legibility, not neuroscience**: we borrow the brain's
  map because humans already know how to read it.
- **Rule 7 holds absolutely:** the Brain Glass is a *projection of the same
  heartbeat data* the orrery renders — a fourth view (orrery · spacetime ·
  ledger · brain), never a second truth. Brain-shaped layout is a template
  property (0009): universes declare a `layout: brain` and a region map;
  everything else falls out of data the glass already receives.
- brain 2's outward axons are kept as the *federation* motif — cross-universe
  connections leaving the skull (0022 Phase 3's picture, when Iroh lands).

## 6. The safety boundary and the success measures

- **Assistive, not clinical, v0** — reminders, recall, orientation,
  communication support, caregiver coordination. The regulated set (diagnosis,
  decline detection, treatment, clinical alerts) lives only in the separate
  Clinical universe, designed with a partner, against FDA CDS guidance. The
  split is architectural so the boundary never drifts (the review's sharpest
  governance insight, adopted whole).
- **The objective vector** (0004's dial, this template's values): correct-recall
  rate · unsupported-memory rate (the one that must be ~zero) · correction rate
  · successful orientation events · reduced repeated questioning · caregiver
  burden · false-alarm rate · consent adherence · provenance completeness.
  Grace's evidence stream (0028/0031) reads these — the improver in a continuity
  universe optimizes for *dignity*, measurably.
