The constitution already answers this — 0039 named the two books (the
Chronicle: purpose data; the Canon: what Orreth runs on) and built the
classes, the retention regimes, the time dial, the metabolism, the
graduation. The drift, owned: **the glass never wore the books**, and the
demo Chronicle is synthetic — canned objectives no one cares about. A
perfect separation over noise still reads as soup. The 0049 legibility
season sanded the soup's words while three of its own P1 threads sat
untouched.

The correction in one line: **a real purpose writing a real Chronicle, the
two books visible on every card, memory proven against it.** Orreth is the
Machine; the Chronicle is what Orreth *does*. This dive makes the second
exist so the first can finally be told apart from it.

## 2. The three steps

### sp1 — the Chronicle gets a real purpose (the keystone)

ONE Trading Desk, sliced thin — not the flagship's three desks and studios:

- **one watchlist** (contents = JB's lock L1),
- **one standing daily objective** — the desk observes, analyzes, and writes,
- **one morning report** JB actually wants to read — the report IS the
  daily proof-walk.

Why this purpose: it generates agentic outcomes the human cares about
*daily*; its data is real-world and time-shaped (what the time dial and
metabolism were built for); each day's report naturally *retrieves* prior
days ("what changed since last week"); CortexObserver is the ground truth
to meet or beat (0049 §4's bar, inherited). Market data arrives through
the Farm's gateway under an explicit standing budget (lock L2 — the Tavily
lesson as law: a standing spend is declared, ceilinged, and cancellable,
never an accident).

**Boundary law, stated up front: the desk observes and reports. It never
executes a trade, moves money, or holds credentials to anything that can.**

### sp2 — every card wears its book

The classes exist (canon.py, 0039 sp1); the glass never used them. Two
changes:

- every card, record, and request in the Console carries a **Chronicle or
  Machine chip** — which book, at a glance;
- the **Inbox defaults to the Chronicle side** (purpose questions, plan
  approvals, reports); Machine housekeeping — joins, drift notes, witness
  alarms, service re-approvals — folds into its own drawer with safe bulk
  decisions.

This is days, not a dive, and it hands the human the missing language:
**a card that cannot say which book it belongs to is, by definition, the
defect.**

### sp3 — the memory program (the goal, named as the dive)

After the desk has run for a week or two, the proven instruments point at
real data — nothing here is new machinery:

- **Allocations**: the canon census per floor — does the desk's floor hold
  what locality law says it should; what rose, what distilled, loss on the
  record. The steward's **live scheduled metabolism beat** goes live here —
  0039 sp3's one honest deferral, discharged.
- **Retrievals**: **JB's ten canonical questions** (lock L4 — they must be
  his words), scored weekly by vera's assay across the RAG flavors as arms
  (0038/0043 as the bench). Recall quality becomes a standing, not an
  anecdote.

## 5. Honest boundary

- One desk, one watchlist — breadth is the flagship's, later.
- Reports only; execution of trades is out of scope forever on this rig.
- sp3's standings measure recall against JB's questions, not universal
  retrieval quality.
- The two-book chip classifies by record class; records that predate
  classing wear their best-effort class, honestly marked.
