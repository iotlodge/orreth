## 2. The problem — what the program's recons already told us

1. **Three harnesses exist at three altitudes, none complete.** The tournament grades by
   string arithmetic and never measured latency or cost (0065 fixes the axes); the
   yardstick is live and LLM-judged but memory-only (ten questions, one lane); vera's
   assay loop is dial-gated and honest — but samples only two rungs of four (0066 builds
   the third) and **nobody judges the judges**: a drifting judge quietly reshapes every
   standing it touches.
2. **"Test as each type" has no per-variant estate.** 0065 staged the law suites (laws,
   never scores, in conformance) and the eleven-arm tournament; what's missing is the
   *quality* bench — per-variant question sets, graded on the signal vector, producing
   standings a human can act on — and the discipline that ties a config version to its
   evidence.
3. **Production monitoring is designed in pieces** — the instrument tier exists
   (declared retention, backdated-reading refusal), degradation cards exist, the drift
   watchdog exists, the bell rings under standing consent — but the E-RAG metric set
   (retrieval success, hallucination alerts, P50/P95, cost per query, *drop over time*)
   has no declared home tying them together.
4. **The security test is a name, not a program.** JB's intent is on the record; rules of
   engagement, targets, timing, and who swings are not.

## 3. The shape

### 3.1 The three-tier test estate (plain words first)

**In plain words: every variant is tested three ways — laws that must never break (run on
every change, free), quality benches that produce scores a human reads (run on a cadence,
judged), and outside yardsticks that let us compare ourselves to the world (run
periodically, cited publicly).**

- **Tier 1 — the law suites** (0065 sp4, matured here): per-variant conformance tests
  asserting the four laws (cited · rebuild-identical · purge-silent · one-truth) plus each
  style's delta, deterministic, in CI, green or nothing.
- **Tier 2 — the quality bench**: per-variant question sets as shelf assets (the
  yardstick's proven pattern, one set per style, each question matched to the style's
  reason for existing), run through the *real* ask lane, graded by the two-tier bench on
  the signal-vector axes (0066: rung-decomposed judge scores + citation coverage +
  latency + cost), landing as standings records the Observatory renders and the router's
  learning reads. The nightly replay (0066) and the bench share one corpus discipline.
- **Tier 3 — the outside yardsticks** (staged as L1): RAGAS-style metrics and selected
  public datasets matched to styles (multi-hop sets for Reasoning-First and Multi-Agent,
  long-document sets for Hierarchical, multilingual sets for the new default embedder) —
  run on a paid cadence, never in CI, their numbers grounding the enterprise story and
  the articles in comparisons a stranger can check.

### 3.2 The judge is also judged

The openwiki research's sharpest lesson, made law: **a small human-labeled gold set**
(JB's own gradings — the thumb machinery already collects them) against which every judge
bench is scored on a cadence. A judge drifting from the gold is *news* — a degradation
card, never a silent reshaping of standings. The existing calibration beat (human-vs-
machine gap, min-n, the bar) extends naturally: judge-vs-gold is the same arithmetic
pointed at the bench. Two-tier wiring stands: the cheap bench carries routine volume
under its ceiling dial; the strong bench takes promotions, regressions, and anything a
human will act on — always from another floor, never its own.

### 3.3 Evidence-or-nothing versioning (staged as L3)

The Compare tab's discipline generalized to law: **no variant configuration version
promotes without its bench evidence attached** — the proposal card carries the before/
after standings, the replay-bench regression check, and the judge tier that graded it.
The gate can still say yes to anything; it just never says yes *blind*. (Law suites
guard CI the same way: a red suite blocks the release ceremony, as it always has.)

### 3.4 Production monitoring, given its one home

The E-RAG metric set lands as declared instrument-tier series with retention and
thresholds as dials: retrieval success · answer helpfulness (the thumbs) · hallucination
alerts (judge-flagged) · latency P50/P95 per lane · throughput · error rate · cost per
query and per person (showback's twin) · cache hit rate · **drop-over-time** (the drift
watchdog pattern pointed at quality series). Cards stage at thresholds; the bell rings
under standing consent; the Monitoring room renders the operate view; the Observatory
holds the verdicts. Nothing new is invented — the organs exist; this dive declares the
set and wires the thresholds.

### 3.5 The security test program (staged as L2 — JB's intent, made a program)

**In plain words: once the doors from Dive VII are built, we attack our own universe the
way a stranger would — on the record, under rules, with every finding becoming a wound
that gets paid.**

- **Targets:** becky's door (nonce replay, imposter DIDs, the delegate-credential chain),
  the queue's new authentication (forge attempts, status abuse), leases (attenuation
  escapes, expiry races, fuel manipulation via the now-locked meter door), the one-face
  law (can a prober distinguish *anything* — endpoints, DIDs, timing), the rate limiter,
  the guardrail proof requirement (serve-without-pin attempts, stale-pin replay, cache
  poisoning across guardrail versions), and the package install road (malicious box,
  tampered signature, crew escape).
- **Two hands:** an **in-house red agent** — a governed adversarial persona in the
  quinn tradition, permanently commissioned, whose *job* is to knock wrong on every door
  on a cadence and file findings as signed records (the universe attacking itself
  honestly, forever — continuous pressure, not a one-time audit); plus a **manual deep
  pass** (JB + Claude, tools in hand) at each security-relevant build milestone.
- **Rules of engagement:** attacks on the dev rig and stranger-shaped deployments, never
  the public demo; every attempt on the record (the red agent's own worldline); findings
  triage into wounds → dives → the honest-boundary register; the article ("The API You
  Cannot Peek At") cites the runs.
- **Timing:** the program's design lands here; the first full pass fires after 0071's
  build spoonfuls land (the doors must exist to be attacked).
