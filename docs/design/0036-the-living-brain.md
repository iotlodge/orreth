# 0036 — The Living Brain (the activity made visible)

*Design draft — proposed by Fable 5 (design owner), from JB's 2026-07-17 ask:
"a highly visual and real-time understanding of all going on in universe/brain
… I want to see what Fable 5 can really do." This is the fun-list dive: the
Brain Glass (0034 §5) taken from anatomy to physiology. JB granted creative
license and one hard requirement — humans will run this fullscreen to read
good flows and backups AS THEY HAPPEN. Rule 7 governs absolutely: every light
is a projection of records and requests the glass already receives — a fourth
sense for one truth, never a second truth.*

---

## Why this dive

The Brain Glass proved the universe has a body. This dive gives it a
physiology: not just *where* the organs live but *what the tissue is doing* —
knowledge arriving, thought fanning, memory consolidating, and above all the
one thing a fullscreen operator most needs at a glance: **where consequence is
waiting for a human**. The design goal in one sentence: *a person who knows
nothing of Orreth should watch for sixty seconds and correctly say "that
region is working, that one is starving, and something up front needs a
person's hand."*

## 1. What I would want to see, as a user of the Brain

Asked plainly, answered plainly — seven senses, each grounded in data the
glass already holds:

1. **Provenance beams — knowledge entering the skull.** A gather lands as a
   beam that BEGINS where the knowledge came from and ENDS at the seat that
   asked. External sources (tavily, a remote MCP — any `did:web:` beyond the
   rig) enter from OUTSIDE the glass, piercing the skull along the federation
   axon paths; internal tools rise from within the tissue. The beam wears the
   trust ladder: **quarantined arrivals are pale blue and flicker** (unproven
   light), **promotion warms them to gold** on later sweeps, and a **recalled
   source's beam replays ashen** when its lineage walk runs. One glance:
   what's feeding this mind, from where, and how much of it is proven.
2. **The synapse gap — HITL pressure made anatomical.** The defining visual.
   A staged request is a signal that TRAVELED and then STOPPED — so draw
   exactly that: sparks race toward the prefrontal (governance) territory and
   halt at a visible cleft short of it, where they ORBIT and PULSE, waiting.
   The waiting sparks accumulate: one is a firefly, five are a halo, twelve
   are an amber **pressure ring** that makes the whole region throb slowly —
   an ache, not an alarm (0024's lesson: alarm fatigue kills lane systems).
   Silence in the queue = the cleft is dark. Click the throb → the Requests
   queue, filtered to WAITING. The metaphor is the mechanism: **HITL is the
   one synapse only a human can fire.**
3. **Flow direction, legible.** Thought traffic already streaks the chain;
   give it grammar: requests DESCEND cool and thin, results RISE warm and
   thick. A healthy universe reads as a circulation; a starved floor reads as
   thin cold threads with nothing warm returning — the "backups" JB wants
   visible are literally visible as one-way traffic.
4. **Consolidation — memory forming, not just landing.** Bursts mark
   arrivals today; add the pyramid's breath: when a distillation runs,
   several embers' light CONVERGES into one brighter point (many raws → one
   distilled, 0003 made visible), and when the purge walks, the light goes
   out point by point with the stubs left as cold pinpricks — erasure with
   receipts, visibly.
5. **Metabolism — the meter as blood flow.** Governed thought costs; let the
   region that is thinking GLOW warmer while its meter runs (0019's per-agent
   spend, subtree-aggregated). A quiet continuity brain breathes dim and
   slow; an enterprise under load flushes. Honest zeros stay dark.
6. **The gain dial.** One control, four stops — **off · calm · living ·
   storm** — scaling particle budget, label density, and ticker verbosity.
   Calm is the bedside default (0034's quiet glass); storm is the operator
   fullscreen. The dial is presentation only — it never filters WHICH truths
   render, only how loudly (rule 7: the projection may whisper, never omit).
7. **The ticker — the quiet caption of what just flashed.** A one-line
   stream under the glass naming each event as it fires: `tavily ⟶
   librarian · 3 findings, quarantined` · `gate: consent waits (2m)` ·
   `f:prod distilled 14 → 1`. Every line clicks through to its record. The
   animation is the feel; the ticker is the receipt.

**And one replay trick, because the data is already there:** the spacetime
drawer holds the record stream — a **replay scrub** plays the last hour of
fire at 60× inside the brain. Operators arriving after an incident watch the
backup FORM, not just its aftermath.

## 2. Where the light comes from (rule 7, enforced by construction)

No new truth channels. Every sense above maps to reads the console already
performs or trivially extends:

| Sense | Source of record |
|---|---|
| provenance beams | recent records via the viewer capability (0030): kind `knowledge`/tags `gather`, source DID in body/lineage — external = non-rig DID |
| synapse gap | `/requests` statuses the glass polls every breath — staged/pending per kind, age = `at` |
| flow direction | request/resolve pairs + run records riding the beat |
| consolidation | distillation records (`derived_from` fan-in) · purge stubs |
| metabolism | the meter totals already on the header, per-scope via `/topology` |
| ticker + replay | the same record stream, newest-first / time-scrubbed |

The worker adds nothing but honesty already owed; the glass adds no
privileged path — the viewer capability reads, the brain renders blind.

## 3. On the orrery — the chart and the mind

JB asked whether cooler representations should replace the orrery. The
design-owner's answer: **the brain becomes the hero view of a living
universe; the orrery remains the chart.** They answer different questions —
the orrery answers *what exists and how it nests* (a map, best for building
and navigating); the brain answers *what is happening and where it hurts*
(an instrument, best fullscreen). Proposal: the Universe view opens on the
brain when a universe has ≥1 template with `layout: brain` or on JB's dial,
with the orrery one click away — and both keep every rule-7 obligation.
Rejected: replacing the orrery outright (navigation by clicking gyri is
charming and unusable), and a third simultaneous view (two truths projected
twice is enough surface to keep honest).
