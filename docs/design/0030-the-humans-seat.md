## Why this is THE keystone

Orreth's claim is not "agents that work." It is: **a human can stand at any point
in time and see, in incredible detail, everything their universe did and why** —
this week's live objective and the full report of one finished thirty days ago.
That claim is only achievable if the universe's work is *indexed by its own
ladder* — not logged, indexed. The brain dives (0022–0029) built the organs. This
dive builds the spine the human climbs, and corrects the deepest drift found so
far: the glass grew as an organ dashboard, when it must be **the human's seat**.

JB: *"We have to get your understanding and my Vision in sync or it simply won't
come together to achieve the claims of Orreth. IT WILL WORK."*

## 1. The ladder — canon, in four rungs

| Rung | Only originated by | What it is | Exists today as |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Objective** | **the human** | "build X · acquire knowledge of Y · check on Z" — submitted at the pane where the human stands; the origin artifact everything below cites | 0027 `kind:"objective"` |
| **Intention** | residents, inside an approved plan | the planned units of delivery — an intention may be assigned to a field, an ecosystem, or the universe itself as a node | 0027 "slivers" (renamed by this dive) |
| **Observation** | workforce (the chassis) | one unit of noticing/doing toward an intention — a skill call or a reasoned step (0015's OBSERVE) | chassis nucleus |
| **Thought** | minds (the Stable) | one governed, metered model call — the atom of cognition, priced and on the record | 0016/0019 meter + RunRecords |

Tiered exactly like the real world, and each rung **reviews the rung below**
(0005 — nothing grades its own yardstick): observations are judged by the critic,
intentions by the dispatching seat, objectives confirmed only by their human.

## 3. The plan gate — the missing HITL stage

Today an objective fans immediately (0027). Canon per JB: **a resident
optimizes/plans/curates the Objective into a plan — and the plan is
HITL-approved before any intention fans.**

`objective arrives → curated into a PLAN (intentions listed: target environments,
budgets, questions it will ask) → plan STAGES for the human (0012 — consequence
waits) → approval fans the intentions → completions ride UP for review → the
origin tier fulfills the Objective → the human's request resolves.`

The severity lanes (R6) may someday auto-approve low-graded plans; **v0: origin
plans always wait for their human.**

## 4. The seat — the glass rebuilt around place

- **Place as the spine.** The human is always AT a floor. The URL carries it
  (back button and bookmarks work); every tab, drawer, dialog, and workspace
  renders that scope **and says so** ("this view · u:demo/e:cloud" on every
  surface); conversations survive navigation.
- **The Objective ledger** — a first-class tab, and the artifact-of-artifacts
  view: submit in plain language → the staged plan (readable, approvable) → the
  live tree (every intention's environment, status, markers as they land) → the
  completed report (everything done, made, decisioned, discovered — each element
  a link into the record) → **recallable forever**. Five concurrent objectives
  are five clean threads, not sixty queue rows.
- **The decision inbox ≠ the traffic log.** "Waiting for you" (staged plans,
  joins, questions, entitlements, high-lane proposals, held purges) separates
  from the ambient log; both sectioned by floor.
- **Scoped tabs + cascade affordances.** Farm/Stable sectioned by floor, the
  human's place first. Planting/saddling at a high floor offers "also to the
  floors below" (button or NLP) — at an ecosystem, you get the ecosystem's view.
- **A legible spacetime window.** Beads a human can see and click — markers,
  moments, objective milestones — notations at human scale; the apex lane tells
  the distilled story, never an empty line.
