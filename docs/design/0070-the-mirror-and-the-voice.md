## 2. The problem — what the survey found

1. **The profile is written, labeled, withdrawable — and read by no one who answers.**
   Sovereignty is real (human assertions enter trusted; inferences must name evidence;
   forgetting is consent-then-shred), the Mirror genuinely assesses conversations into
   observations — but the grounding kit that reaches the model carries **no profile
   slice**. 0025 deferred exactly this ("per-resident personalization adoption") and it
   was never collected.
2. **Conversations do not exist as memory.** An exchange record carries a client-minted
   random session string and **no lineage** — every audience record is an orphan; the
   thread lives in the browser and dies when the pane closes; no reply has ever seen an
   earlier turn (every model call in the repository is single-turn). Meanwhile the
   worldline idiom the fix needs is proven five times over.
3. **The persona layer is a Rust constant.** Every resident wears the identical
   resident-voice prompt (identity, place, a length cap, a grounding rule — no
   disposition, no expertise); the only character text is hardcoded blurbs in the plane's
   source. And the capability residents already shame the kernel's own: charles, charlene,
   and chad carry shelf-borne, version-controlled, ref-cited personas today.
4. **"Last Tuesday" is unparseable and beliefs can't ride the clock.** The time dial
   needs ISO dates, is never called from the worker's answer path, and profile records
   are barred from the projection by the privacy floor. For *beliefs*, versions-as-time
   (replaying a lineage chain to a cutoff) is the truthful primitive — and it exists in
   the knowledge module already.
5. **The kernel does not know which human is speaking.** One seat key per floor: a thumb,
   a preference, a profile is per-*floor*, not per-*person* — the old 0012 signer-registry
   debt, and a wall the proof's enterprise reality (authority-scoped answers, multi-human
   estates) runs straight into.

## 3. The shape

### 3.1 Conversations become worldlines (plain words first)

**In plain words: a conversation becomes a real thread the machine remembers — each turn
linked to the one before it, the whole thread findable later — instead of a picture the
browser repaints and forgets.**

- Audience records gain lineage (`derived_from` to the prior turn — the proven idiom);
  a session becomes a first-class thread with a head; the truncation caps become dials
  with the full turns riding bodies.
- **Replies see the thread**: the voiced lane composes prior turns (recent turns whole,
  older turns as the memory-retriever pattern the scope notes teach — rewrite the ask
  with memory, then retrieve), within a token budget. This is the Memory-Augmented
  variant's substrate, built where every channel shares it.
- The scrub lands honestly twice: **events** scrub by the time dial (grown a small
  deterministic relative-date parser, with the 0066 analyzer's escalation pattern for
  ambiguity); **beliefs** scrub by versions-as-time — replay the claim's lineage to the
  cutoff, which is what "what did we believe Tuesday" truly asks.

### 3.2 The profile is finally read

- A **profile slice** enters the grounding kit through the librarian's sovereign door
  (the privacy floor stands — profile records never enter any projection or corpus;
  they are injected structurally, provenance-labeled: *you told me / I observed / the
  mirror noticed*).
- **Preferences become typed claims**: `{"prefers": {language, verbosity, …}}`,
  human-asserted therefore trusted, correctable by re-assertion, withdrawable by
  consent. The answer-language preference (0069's hook) lands here and the voiced lane
  honors it.
- The Mirror keeps its seat and its hygiene; its observations now *reach* the voice
  instead of only the portrait panel.

### 3.3 The persona layer — 0046's promise, collected under JB's razor word

- **Per-resident Chronicle craft**, worn via a `⟦persona⟧` slot at the one prompt-assembly
  choke point every resident's voice already passes through. The **Universe default
  persona** (helpful, not opinionated, professional) is the base asset every resident
  inherits; each resident's persona is expertise-reflective and stays helpful, clear,
  honest — the doctrine's dials (disposition · register with cryptic out of range ·
  expertise voice · opinion stance · the inherited tighten-only honesty floor) as the
  asset's declared shape.
- **The razor amendment (staged as L2, JB's charter word made kernel law):** persona

  siblings, for *all* residents including the kernel's — while ability prompts (what a
  voice may *do*) remain firmware. The prod razor's carve-out is explicit, never
  implied.
- **The Rust blurbs retire honorably**: served from the shelf with the literal as
