## The problem, and why it is almost free

`the-objective-atlas.md` is the standing interop map — on paper, kept honest
only by a maintenance law that depends on discipline. Meanwhile the machine
already holds everything a live map needs: the glass has a blind `flow`
renderer (0055's panel vocabulary — the desks ship their sixteen-stage graphs

capability manifest, and the governance loops are known shapes that simply
have never been *declared as data*. LangGraph gives one flow a `get_graph()`;
this dive gives the whole universe one — **and its graph includes the
humans.**

## The two honesty laws

- **The shape is declared; the life is measured.** The governance and human
  flow declarations plant as shelf craft (`atlas-governance-flow`,
  `atlas-human-flow`) — the machine's own schematic is a governed, editable
  part of the machine, tunable at the gates like the dictionary; capability
  flows come from the manifests that already declare them. Edge-lighting
  comes only from real record activity — no edge ever glows that did not
  carry something.
- **Every box is a door** (0052): an organ opens its room, a gate opens the
  Inbox, a world opens its rooms, the composer opens Objectives. The map is
  never a poster; it is navigation.
