# 0060 — The First Contact

*Opened 2026-08-25, from the first-contact design talk. All four locks landed
by JB's hand the same morning, every one on the recommended path.*

## The problem

Three quinn-web walks (req-1193 · req-1218 · req-1241) converged on a finding
the plain-speech waves could not cure: the remaining frictions were not bad
copy but **the canon vocabulary itself meeting a stranger** — ecosystem,
floor, agent, the `u:/e:/f:` address grammar, canary, saddled, the stable
metaphor, "who is chad beyond a name." The register is load-bearing (saddled
≠ configured; the pasture IS the EOL calendar) and it is the product's voice.
The fork was never *poetry vs corporate*.

## The locks (JB, 2026-08-25)

1. **Strategy: teach + diet.** Keep the canon register; build the teaching
   apparatus, and demote only pure-friction edges that carry no load.
2. **The glossary is shelf craft, and every word is a door.** Definitions
   plant as `gloss-*` assets on the 0050 speech shelf — versioned, editable
   at the gates like any sentence. 0052's law (*every mention is a door*)
   extends from record-refs to vocabulary: click a canon word anywhere and
   its definition opens where you stand. Static tooltips were refused by
   name — they are the exact wound class waves 3–4 killed (an explanation
   only a hovering hand can find is invisible to a scanning stranger and to
   the UAT eye alike).
3. **The welcome: a five-stop band, never an overlay.** Shown until
   dismissed, brought back by the header's `?`: the rail is the world · the
   Inbox waits on you · residents are staff you can talk to · Capabilities
   are installed worlds · start anything by asking in Objectives. It adds a
   row of words; it covers nothing.
4. **First home: the rig, then the demo refresh** — build and prove here,
   then demo.orreth.ai (where strangers actually land) meets it.

## The mechanism

- `orreth_sim/speech.py` grows `GLOSSARY` (~35 entries, each a plain
  definition under 240 chars, no slots, no design-doc citations —
  suite-held). They plant at boot beside the sentences; the craft room
  lists them; the one-motion edit door tunes them.
- The worker's `/sentences` door serves a `gloss` map — **active heads
  first, genesis beneath** — so an edited definition speaks at once.
- The glass wraps the **first occurrence per render** of each word in a
  dotted door (`.gw`), text nodes only (0052's law — attributes and
  handlers are never touched), and a click opens a small card naming the
  word, its definition, and where to tune it. Aliases fold inflections
  (memories→memory, field→floor, fingerprint→pin…).
- **The rail diet**: the `e:`/`f:` prefixes leave the rail's display — the
  colored dots and indentation already carry the kind; full addresses stay
  in tooltips, breadcrumbs, and detail views. The legend shrinks to plain
  words; the header stats label themselves.

## The closing measure

quinn-web walks as a true first-timer (headless Chrome carries no
localStorage, so she always meets the welcome band). The dive closes when
her lists stop naming undefined canon words and start judging content —
plus the human check in the glass.
