*The universe learns to measure what it actually does.*

## 0. The law

Every observability product is a third person: an external witness watching a
system through exported, unsigned trace blobs, judging them in a foreign court
— someone else's SaaS, someone else's retention, someone else's judges. Orreth
refuses the sidecar, because it holds an advantage no sidecar can copy: **the
trace already exists, and it is signed.** An Objective decomposing into
Intentions, each Intention meeting Observations, each Thought metered and
chained by `derived_from` — that is a governance-grade distributed trace that
the industry approximates with JSON and hope.

So the constitution extends, rather than bends:

> **Observability is a projection over the signed log — never a second truth
> (rule 7), never a federation. Evaluation is a seat that is not the actor
> (rule 2). Depth costs money and says so (0019). And the instruments render
> in the same glass as everything they watch.**

Other stacks film the robot. Here, the world remembers — and the Observatory
reads.

## 2. The organ — vera, the astronomer

**LOCKED (JB, 2026-07-30): the Observatory gets its own resident.**

vera — for Vera Rubin, who measured what galaxies actually did against what
their visible matter claimed, and found the invisible mass. She keeps the
Observatory. Her laws:

1. **She is the inverse of vigil.** vigil is content-*blind* detection; vera
   is content-*aware* assay. The separation is what keeps vigil trustworthy
   and vera useful; the two never merge.
2. **She never grades her own floor's homework.** Her judges are always
   another floor's minds (the graduation pipeline's independence, made a
   standing law). Her verdicts land as signed records under the judges'
   authorship, scribe-witnessed — author ≠ agent, always.
3. **Detection wears no levers** (0041 §4, inherited whole). A finding —
   a degrading score, a drifting distribution, a losing experiment arm —
   stages at a human gate. vera measures; humans move.
4. **Her own cost is her first exhibit.** Every assay she commissions rides
   the meter under her DID; the Observatory's price is always one of its own
   instruments.

## 3. The storage law — two tiers

**LOCKED (JB, 2026-07-30): two-tier.**

- **Assay records** — judge verdicts, standings, experiment results, human
  gradings — are **signed Chronicle records**. They are institutional
  experience; they feed the 0039 evidence→Canon loop directly; they are
  never pruned, only distilled.
- **Instrument series** — per-call latencies, counters, rates, distributions
  — are a **projection with declared retention**: rebuildable from the log
  wherever they claim log-truth, and labeled *"instrument reading, not
  testimony"* where they are performance-derived. And the metabolism applies
  to its own telemetry: raw series distill to hourly, hourly to daily, with
  measured loss, like every other memory. **Even the monitoring ages
  honestly.**

## 4. The taps

Rule 5 makes this dive cheap, because the choke points already exist:

- **The model plane gateway** (the stable's LiteLLM-direct door): every
  resident and workforce thought already passes through it to be metered.
  The meter extends into a flight recorder — latency, model id, tokens
  in/out, refusal taxonomy, per governed thought. An organ that thinks
  off-meter is drift; therefore coverage is total *by construction*.
- **The farm gateway** (charlotte): every tool call — volume, latency, error
  rate, per service worldline. Rug-pull correlation falls out for free.
- **The fingertip**: flow nodes gain span timing (start/end/status riding
  the choreography records that already exist).
- **The doors**: gate-wait ages — how long consequence waited for a human is
  a first-class metric, and almost nobody in the industry measures it.
- **The beats**: epoch, metabolism, serials — they already emit; vera reads.

## 5. The dial — glance · watch · assay

The brain's gain dial, reused for depth-of-instrumentation (JB's toggle):

- **glance** — free: series and counters only.
- **watch** — spans, distributions, percentiles.
- **assay** — the Examiner beat turns on: vera samples completed work on a
  cadence and commissions independent judges, on a budget the meter shows to
  the decimal. This is "online evaluation" told honestly: quality costs
  money, and the dial says how much.

## 6. The assay loop

On the beat (assay dial only): sample recent completed Intentions/Objectives
→ route each to a judge in another floor's stable with the rubric its
Objective declared → verdicts land as signed records → standings aggregate →
degradations stage findings at the gate. Human gradings enter the same shelf
by the same shape (a card, a verdict, a signature) — LangSmith's annotation
queue collapses into a gate we already have. The Mirror remains the
conversation-assessor; vera assays *work*; neither grades itself.

## 7. The experiment — A/B where arms are machines

The crown jewel, and mostly assembly:

1. Two Canon variants planted as versioned assets (grace's lanes, 0031).
2. **Each arm is an Epoch** — a signed, named machine (0041). No metadata
   tagging, no "did we record the config?" — the arm *is* the fingerprint.
3. The dispatcher splits traffic by a declared, human-approved policy.
4. Standings accumulate per-arm (0038's machinery, generalized beyond rows).
5. The conclusion is not a dashboard. It is a **promotion card at the
   human's gate**, the losing standard versioned behind the winner, the
   adoption naming its evidence (the 0038 orphan fix's lineage law).

*A/B testing where each arm is a cryptographically named machine and the
rollout is a signed constitutional act.* That is the sentence we market.
