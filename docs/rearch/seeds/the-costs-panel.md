## JB's word, faithfully

"The old world tracked everything — the meter in dollars, tokens per body — and the new main view
does not show ACCUMULATED costs/tokens. Not a big deal … lots of ideas … aggregating the metrics into
a Costs Panel, down the road."

## What stands today (the aggregation lives; the glass owes the panel)

- The meter is universal and in dollars (P6.5 sp3): every thought a body has rides the gateway
  under its own key and lands a `spine_meter` line — DID · model · tokens · usd · landed-at · stall ·
  ok. `stable.spend` rolls it up per body; a mind card on the CREW pull says what a mind has cost
  over how many calls; the PERFORMANCE layer of THE PANEL shows the spend beside the doors' p95.
- What is missing is the PANEL of it: one place that says what this world has spent, accumulated,
  by day · by body · by mind · by intention, against the fuel clauses and the ceilings, with the
  trend — the old world's rollup (`orreth-rollup`, 0005's monoidal StatBundle: costs summed up the
  tree, floors flagged never averaged away) drawn for a human.

## The spirit carried forward (from the old world, at the tag `main-v0.72-old-world`)

- **0005's rollup law**: cost is a monoid — component-wise sums (tokens · model calls · usd ·
  wall) that merge up a chain without double counting; a breach anywhere is a breach of the whole.
  The panel is that fold drawn, per world and — when the seam carries it — across cells.
- **0058's fuel clause**: allowance-per-window with lazy renewal; the panel shows every key's
  allowance, what is left, when it renews, and the refill door beside it.
- **The intent (0007)**: COST is one of the five intentions that control SCALE (JB's one law of
  understanding); the panel is where a cost intention reads its own ground.

## Where it slots

A fourth soft toggle on THE PANEL (geometry · light · performance · **costs**), fed by the meter's
aggregates on both kernels (`/minds/spend` widened to the fold above), a fixture `costs-v0.json`
pinning the fold. Nothing new is metered; only the reading is new. Not before 0.1.0.
