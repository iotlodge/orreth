# 0001 — The Proofs Era

**Status:** accepted (JB's word, 2026-09-04)

## Context

Sixty-five design dives (`0000`–`0064`) took the kernel from a vision to a running, public,
stranger-runnable estate: the reference proves the model (371 conformance tests), the Rust plane
runs it, the book teaches it, the demo shows it, and capabilities install as folders of
declarations. The standing risk of this moment is the one every substrate faces after its
foundation season: growing speculative surface — machinery built because it *could* be, not
because a purpose demanded it.

## Decision

**From here on out, the work is proofs.** A proof is a purpose world installed on the kernel and
exercised end to end — embodiment, fleets, attestation, governed updates, energy budgets, and
whatever purposes follow — each one maturing the kernel by *using* it.

Two disciplines follow:

1. **New dives default to proof-shaped work**: a purpose world, its walk, and the honest register
   of what the walk demanded.
2. **Kernel and firmware changes land in service of a proof's honest need** — a wound a proof
   actually hit — never as speculative surface. The razor stands: capabilities are the human's
   domain; changing how the machine is governed is a release.

## Consequences

- The proof builds land as sibling purpose worlds/repos on the same kernel, governed by the same
  firmware, without a line of kernel change — and where a proof *does* demand a kernel change,
  that demand is the evidence the change was real.
- The README's "Next" carries the era; the covenant carries the rule (its enforcement card,
  rule 12).
- The design canon's dive numbering continues; proofs get dives like any other keystone work.
