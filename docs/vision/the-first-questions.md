## The first questions of every incident

Any incident, any domain, the first five minutes are always the same questions. In today's
enterprises — and catastrophically in today's agentic deployments — every one of them starts an
*investigation*. In an Orreth universe, every one of them is a **lookup**:

| The question | The lookup |
|---|---|
| **"What is it?"** | its **birth certificate** (0011): archetype, generation, skill versions at stamp, who ordered it, when in the universe's life it was born |
| **"What does it do?"** | its skill bindings and objective vector — and its **RunRecords**: not what it was supposed to do, what it *measurably did* |
| **"What's the blast radius?"** | the one nobody else can answer. Its **possible** reach is its capability chain — attenuation-only, so the ceiling is cryptographic, not aspirational. Its **actual** reach is its signed access log. Possible and actual, both readable, in minutes |
| **"What's going on?"** | the **spacetime window**, scrubbed to now — and backward to when it started. vigil's signals show the shape of the anomaly without waiting for content forensics |
| **"Who do I need to contact?"** | the identity chain: sponsor, lease, issuing becky, the tenant's GOD — every hop signed, every hop a name |
| **"Who — or what — is causing this?"** | **Sourced + Verified authorship on every record**, and the `context_hash` on every run: you can split *the agent misbehaved* from *the policy told it to* — the distinction every post-mortem fights about, pre-answered |

And the question traditional IR can never answer at all: **"what did it know, and when did it
know it?"** The window scrubs the agent's own memory timeline. An incident in an Orreth universe
arrives with its history attached — all the pieces, already assembled, because assembly was
never deferred to the worst possible moment.

> The pitch in one line: **everyone else's incident response begins with archaeology.
> Ours begins with an answer.**

## The Universe Risk Assessment

JB has sat through the current state of the art: interviews, sampled evidence, screenshots of
consoles — "talks and sampling," worthless for understanding actual threat and risk, repeated
separately for every framework. The substrate inverts it:

- **Sampling dies when you can afford the census.** Control testing today samples because
  checking everything is impossible. Roll-ups (0005) carry compliance state over **100% of
  activity** — a floor breach flags the bundle, unmissable, undilutable by volume. The audit
  question "show me evidence this control operated" becomes a standing query, not an annual dig.
- **Controls that are structural don't need testimony.** Separation of duties = quorums that
  refuse below the bar (0012, tested). Records retention = min/max floors the resolver enforces
  (0004). Access governance = attenuation-only tokens from a pinned root (0006). "We can't read
  your data" = key custody math (0013). The assessor doesn't interview anyone about these —
  they *verify* them, the way the conformance suite does.
- **One assessment, every regulator, simultaneously.** NIST, DORA, PCI-DSS, banking, state,
  federal — each regulator holds a **scoped, entitled, audited window** into exactly their
  jurisdiction (`governed-human-oversight.md`), all projections of the *same* substrate. The
  enterprise stops performing five audits a year and starts *hosting* five standing views of
  one truth. Assessment stops being an event and becomes a property.
- **And the assessment is itself accountable.** Every regulator read is a signed access record.
  The watchers are watched — which is precisely what makes deep access grantable at all.

## Why this sells to the Chiefs

The CFO buys fewer audit-hours and a defensible number. The CRO buys blast-radius-in-minutes
and risk quantified over the census, not the sample. The CCO buys five frameworks from one
substrate. The CISO buys the pre-assembled incident timeline. And the board buys the sentence
at the top: **in our agentic enterprise, nothing acts without identity, and nothing is ever
unattributable.** Agentic growth stops being a governance trade-off — the governance is why
you can afford to grow.
