# Orreth — Use Cases
### The same machine, different costumes

*Private vision artifact. Captured 2026-06-30. Companion to `FUTURE-the-orreth.md`.*

> The tell of a real architecture: wildly different domains fall out of the **same primitives.**
> The domain is just a **Tier Profile** + *which primitives you lean on.* Build the primitives once —
> sell into all of them.

Each use case below names the **primitive it proves** and the **design requirement it demands** — because
these aren't just demos, they're the pressure test that tells the substrate keystone what to nail
(consolidated at the end).

**The primitive set** (the corners each costume leans on):
Living Identity · Memory-up-pruned · Policy-down-foundational · Retrieval-by-time-horizon (Sourced + Verified) ·
Branch-and-diverge · Tenant isolation · Skills-as-crystallized-memory · The join spectrum.

## 1. The multi-LOB conglomerate — *the CFO's dream* (the first commercial killer)

- **Map:** Company = **Universe** · each line of business = **Ecosystem** · each core function (pricing, AR, supply chain) = an **Agent**.
- **The moment:** every number that rolls up to the board is **Sourced + Verified** — cryptographically attributable to the function that produced it. That's not BI; that's *audit that can't lie about its own provenance.* The apex runs financial-analysis agents over the all-LOB, all-time corpus, finds the pricing skill that beats the others, and **promotes it to every LOB.**
- **Proves:** aggregation + governance + audit (roll-up · policy-down · skill promotion).
- **Demands:** monoidal roll-up; Sourced+Verified as a first-class audit property. *(The join spectrum doubles as an M&A story: an acquired subsidiary runs **floors-only** — takes corporate security/compliance, keeps its own ops — and graduates to **fully-joined**.)*

## 2. The sports league — *and the trade that proves the model*

- **Map:** League = **Universe** · each team = **Ecosystem** · each player/coach/scout = a **Living Identity** with a career-long, non-fading memory.
- **The moment:** *"how has every left-handed closer performed against this batter across all of history?"* escalates to the Universe (all-time, all-teams), Sourced + Verified. Then a player is **traded** — their **identity and career memory travel with them**, but the old team's **private playbook memory does not** (it was scoped to the old Ecosystem). Identity portability + memory isolation, in one human moment.
- **Proves:** cross-spacetime retrieval + identity portability + isolation.
- **Demands:** memory **portability scope** (portable vs branch-bound); the join spectrum as *competitive secrecy vs league law* (a team must take league rules, opts its playbook out of sharing).

## 3. Healthcare — *the lifelong patient journey*

- **Map:** Health system = **Universe** · hospital/clinic = **Ecosystem** · care function = **Agent** · the patient = a **Living Identity** whose record never fades and follows them across providers **with consent.**
- **The moment:** a clinician retrieves *"every similar presentation across all of history"* (deep-time, all-provider) — Sourced + Verified. And when a patient invokes erasure, the record is **provably retired by a governed tombstone**, not silently lost — auditable to the day, the policy, the person.
- **Proves:** longitudinal memory + provenance + **governed erasure/consent.**
- **Demands:** the retention/tombstone model + consent as a hard, foundational (Universe-level) policy floor; cross-tenant retrieval authorization.

## 4. The trading firm / bank — *regulatory memory*

- **Map:** Firm = **Universe** · desks/regions = **Ecosystems** · strategies + risk-checkers = **Agents**.
- **The moment:** every trade and the market state around it is remembered forever; the apex runs cross-desk risk + regime analysis over all-time. When the regulator asks, the answer is **Sourced + Verified** end to end (MiFID/Dodd-Frank as a Universe policy floor). Risk floors are **non-overridable** by any desk — a rogue desk *cannot* loosen them.
- **Proves:** foundational floors + all-time analysis + audit.
- **Demands:** lexicographic non-overridable floors; time-horizon retrieval (fast local risk vs deep regime history).

## 5. The scientific research consortium — *maybe the most important*

- **Map:** Consortium = **Universe** · labs = **Ecosystems** · experiment-runners = **Agents**.
- **The moment:** **every experiment, forever, Sourced + Verified** — provenance for every result. The reproducibility crisis dies here. The apex does meta-analysis and **hypothesis generation** over all accumulated experimental memory; a successful assay is captured as a **skill** and cascaded to every lab with its rubric + baseline (reproducible protocol propagation).
- **Proves:** provenance + apex-analysis + skills-as-crystallized-memory.
- **Demands:** the rubric/baseline model (0001); Sourced+Verified as scientific integrity.

## 6. The learning universe — *education that never forgets*

- **Map:** School/curriculum = **Universe** · courses or cohorts = **Ecosystems** · tutors + each learner = **Living Identities**.
- **The moment:** a learner's *entire* journey is remembered (the opposite of cram-and-forget); the system runs **branch-and-diverge** on two pedagogies across parallel cohorts and promotes the one that measurably teaches better. A tutoring method that works becomes a **skill** shared to every cohort.
- **Proves:** branch-and-diverge + skill promotion + longitudinal identity.
- **Demands:** identity lineage (a learner identity persisting across courses); branch outcome measurement.

## 7. Supply-chain provenance — *the verified journey of everything*

- **Map:** Network = **Universe** · each partner/region = **Ecosystem** · each node = an **Agent**.
- **The moment:** every part's entire journey is **Sourced + Verified** across partners who don't fully trust each other — the holy grail of traceability — while each partner keeps its internals **tenant-isolated** and takes only the network's shared floors.
- **Proves:** cross-tenant provenance under isolation.
- **Demands:** Sourced+Verified across low-trust tenants; the join spectrum for guarded participation.

## 8. The AI multiverse game — *live all your lives*

- **Map:** Multiverse = **Universe** · each game-"universe" = an **Ecosystem** (a parallel life) · you + the NPCs = **Living Identities** that persist across sessions (reboot ≠ reset).
- **The moment:** the **same agent runs in every branch** (its soul cascades down), but a **life-choice is a Standard targeted at one branch** — inject it, and that life diverges. Later: *"show me how it went in the life where I took the job"* — a Sourced + Verified read across branches.
- **Proves:** branch-and-diverge + identity-across-branches (the counterfactual primitive).
- **Demands:** identity **archetype → incarnation** (one template, many branch-bound incarnations; shared skills, isolated memory).

## 9. The persistent living world — *emergent history*

- **Map:** The world = **Universe** · regions/factions = **Ecosystems** · thousands of AI citizens = **Living Identities** with real, non-fading memory · players = the "gods" who tune policy from above.
- **The moment:** citizens *remember* — grudges, alliances, debts — across years of real time. History **emerges** and is queryable at the apex. The "gods" don't script events; they set **policy floors** and watch the world diverge (Westworld, but the memory is real).
- **Proves:** persistence at scale + policy-down as world-physics + pruning (you can't keep every citizen's every moment — the layers distill it into history).
- **Demands:** aggressive pruning policy; retrieval at scale.

## 10. The personal second brain — *your own memory, un-faded*

- **Map:** You = **Universe** · life domains (work, health, family) = **Ecosystems** · your assistants = **Agents**.
- **The moment:** your whole life is remembered and retrievable across your own spacetime — the human-memory-fades problem, solved *for you.*
- **Proves:** the substrate at n=1.
- **Demands:** the retrieval + retention model at personal scale; airtight single-tenant isolation.

*…and it keeps going: law firms (matters as memory, precedent retrieval across all-time, privilege = isolation),
smart cities (a living municipal record), collaborative fiction (a shared world-memory) — same machine, new costume.*

## The product — **"Build My First Universe"**

Orreth up and running, self-serve. You subscribe and spin up a Universe from a **template** — and the
template *is* an architecture artifact: a **Tier Profile + a starter bundle** (Standards, skill archetypes,
a roster of agent templates, a memory-retention config, and the policy floors that set the tone).

- **Pick a template** — "Company," "League," "Game," "Lab," "Second Brain" — name it, and Orreth provisions the tiers.
- **Wild vs REAL is a policy dial.** A game universe runs unbounded and fantastical; an enterprise universe runs
  grounded, compliant, audited. *Same engine — the policy floors are what make one WILD and the other REAL.*

### The experience that sells it — **interview the agents before you buy**

This is the part a stateless world can't offer. Because agents are **Living Identities with real memory and a
track record**, you can **sit down and interview a candidate before you lease it** — ask about its experience,
test its judgment, probe a skill, watch it think. A job interview, for an AI. The marketplace stops being star
ratings and becomes *"talk to it yourself."*

**The design gem it forces (keep-us-real):** the interview must run in a **showcase scope** — the agent speaks
to its own **portfolio** (its skills and an anonymized/aggregate track record) but **never leaks a prior tenant's
private memory.** You cannot interview a leased agent and extract its former employer's secrets. So every identity
carries two memory scopes: a **public portfolio** (interviewable, Sourced + Verified) and its **tenant-private
memory** (walled). The interview is a *bounded, sandboxed retrieval session* against the portfolio scope only.

That single mechanism gives the marketplace something no marketplace has: **provable trust before purchase** —
AgentFacts (runtime-earned evaluations) + a Sourced/Verified portfolio + a live interview.

## What the use cases demand of the keystone (consolidated requirements)

The costumes agree. These are now hard requirements for the substrate dive:

1. **Identity lineage — archetype → incarnation.** One template identity, many scoped incarnations; shared skills, isolated memory. *(game, conglomerate, league, education)*
2. **Memory portability scope — `portable | branch-bound`.** What travels when an identity moves. *(trade, reorg, new life)*
3. **Cross-branch / cross-tenant retrieval authorization.** Who may read across ecosystems — the #1 security surface. *(compare-my-lives, scout-the-league, benchmark-all-LOBs)*
4. **Showcase scope — public portfolio vs tenant-private memory.** The interview mechanism; the marketplace trust primitive. *(Build My First Universe)*
5. **Retention / governed-tombstone erasure + consent as a foundational floor.** *(healthcare, GDPR)*
6. **Sourced + Verified as a first-class audit property.** *(finance, science, supply chain)*

> Wild on the outside, rigorous on the inside. The use cases prove the vision generalizes — and hand us the spec.
