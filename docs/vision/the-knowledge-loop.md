## The frame

Memory residence and persistent life: **achieved.** What comes next is the *nervous system* —
and JB's decomposition names its two core lenses: **ability** (what the universe's agents can do,
measured) and **adaptation** (how they grow through knowledge and skills, governed). HITL holds
the consequential acts; floors and KMS-bounded worlds hold the containment. The self-correcting
loop already exists in the locked design — act → record → score → distill → crystallize →
cascade → act better (0001 · 0005 · 0007) — but today it learns only from the universe's **own
experience**. The knowledge loop extends it to learn from **the world**.

## The ML insight — flip the slice on its side

*"Take our sliced moment in Universe Time. Flip it on its side and you've got structured data,
every minute, forever."* — JB

What enterprise ML spends fortunes assembling (collect · curate · label · version · trace), an
Orreth universe **secretes as a byproduct of operating**. A RunRecord is already an experience
tuple with receipts — agent, goal, inputs, outcome, per-objective scores — signed, and pinned
via `context_hash` to the exact policy it ran under, so the training data itself separates
*behavior changed* from *the rules changed*. Roll-ups already surface best-score records with
full lineage. The regulatory convergence (EU AI Act–class dataset-provenance duties) makes this
a market: **model cards with a chain of custody, generated as a side effect.**

## The five primitives (the 0014 dive)

1. **The Knowledge Category** — a first-class, versioned-by-universe-time corpus built around an
   intent ("cold-weather architecture strategies"). Timestamps are knowledge versions: *what did
   we know about X, as of T.* A materialized, curated view over memory with a goal attached.
2. **Orreth.agent — the librarian archetype.** The core agent whose intent is the goal it was
   given and whose skill is building and tending Knowledge Categories from external feeds,
   forever. The Second Brain's engine and the enterprise research organ — one archetype, two
   markets.
3. **External sources as identities.** Feeds get DIDs and trust postures (a source registry:
   NOAA, arXiv, vendor APIs). Provenance of the outside world — without it, "verified" stops at
   our walls.
4. **The Dataset as a frozen artifact** — a spacetime query result, content-addressed and pinned
   *before* the metabolism distills its raw inputs, curation lineage as manifest. What training
   consumes; what auditors ask for.
5. **Propagation with recall** *(the self-propagating addition)*. Every observing agent that
   improves a Category mints a new signed version, inherited instantly along authorization
   edges — the network effect inside the universe. The contamination risk is answered
   structurally: **the recall** — `derived_from` chains make every downstream absorber of a
   discredited source enumerable and flaggable, annotate-never-rewrite. Governed lanes gate
   propagation speed; Bayesian confidence makes new knowledge *visible immediately, trusted
   gradually*; **generation depth** (hops from primary sources) is carried on every version so
   consumers can weight and curation can force re-anchoring — the model-collapse failure mode,
   answered before it exists.

Plus the standing debt this all sits on: **LiteLLM through the floors** — the model gateway made
real.

## Topic 3 — the immune system's adaptive arm (captured 2026-07-03)

The first-questions doc gave the **innate** arm: verified threats become floors at ingress.
Topic 3 adds the **adaptive** arm — *poison search and removal* (working marketing name: **the
Recall & Quarantine**; umbrella story: *the universe has an immune system*):

- **Detection through intent and outcome.** Outcome is the empirical lens: 0005's bundles let a
  hunter ask *which knowledge versions do failing runs have in common?* — poison located by its
  symptoms. Intent is the other lens: records whose producing goal mismatches their claim.
- **Enumeration via `derived_from`** — every downstream absorber listable (the recall).
- **Agentic staging, HITL removal** — the hunter stages through the 0012 queues; humans hold the
  delete, per policy. For knowledge, *discredit beats delete* by default: tombstone the body,
  keep the signed stub, cascade redaction markers — history never silently changes.

**The trust lifecycle** (JB's 0.0000 partition): unverified external data (Tavily-class web,
AI-era content) is *admitted quarantined* — alive in time, tagged/attributed, with a lifecycle
**state**: `untrusted (0.0000) → under investigation → corroborated → trusted`, and the poison
arm is the same ladder running down. Investigation is a skill; promotion is earned; the Bayesian
confidence is the math, the state is the workflow. Rookie probation applied to knowledge — one
philosophy, third application. A universe that can *see* the whole web without *believing* it.

**The MCP corollary:** cloud-provider MCPs as identified sources → provider-specific
build/deploy/monitor skills that refresh as docs update → one agent identity, adaptive across
every cloud, the skill shareable to any element of the universe. The product line hiding here:
*a universe's identity IS its accumulated knowledge, skills, and earned trust postures — and you
get to build it.*

## The honest boundaries

- **Verified ≠ true.** The machinery guarantees authorship and integrity, never the truth of an
  external feed's content. Curation skills + count-weighted confidence carry the epistemic load;
  the pitch says *provenance-complete*, never *truth-complete*.
- **Orreth doesn't do the learning.** It makes ML's most expensive input nearly free and fully
  governed. Humbler claim, stronger position.
- **The 90–95% claim** (decision pressure = understanding the inputs) is JB's thirty-year field
  estimate and is presented as exactly that.
